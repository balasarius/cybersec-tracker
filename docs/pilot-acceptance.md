# First-team pilot acceptance

The pilot is successful when a named team can complete the representative Apache remediation lifecycle described in `DESIGN.md` section 19 using synthetic or approved non-production data.

## Required evidence

- Pilot users, Security roles, team ownership rules, task policy, and communication route are configured.
- The same issue is imported from two sources without losing provenance or producing duplicate work.
- An analyst reviews consolidation, approves the OWASP assessment, confirms ownership and releases the task.
- The owner explicitly acknowledges, records progress and submits evidence through the agreed primary interface.
- The platform records follow-up, commitments, target dates, source state, external synchronization, and every privileged decision.
- Jira projection uses summary-plus-link and a remote Done state cannot complete the security task.
- A source disagreement returns to analyst review; accepted evidence permits an authorised analyst to complete the task.
- Simulated recurrence reopens the task without erasing its previous completion.
- Operational reporting reconciles to the task and its complete audit timeline.
- Pilot representatives complete setup and workflow training and record acceptance or actionable gaps.

Quantitative targets for triage response, acknowledgement, successful routing, and usability will be agreed with the named team during pilot planning. Stage 4 cannot exit without human acceptance from Product, Security, Engineering, and the pilot representative.
